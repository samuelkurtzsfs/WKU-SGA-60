What the plaque gets wrong

Corrections and open questions

The names in this archive begin as a claim made by the plaque in the SGA Chambers. People who served have reported that their year on it is wrong, so every plate is treated as something to check. This page is the running account of what has been moved, what is still unsettled, and what has simply not been confirmed yet.

A correction is not an embarrassment. It is the part of the record that shows the work.

Corrections made4

The archive placed these terms somewhere other than the plaque does. The site follows the archive.

Larry Zielke 1969-70

What the plaque reads
1970-71
Where this site places the term
1969-70
Office
President

Plaque reads 1970-71, paired with John Lyne. Herald coverage places Zielke's actual term here, in 1969-70: he and Frank Genzianelli 'clash for the presidency' in the 25 March 1969 special election edition, and by 22 May 1969 the Herald reports Zielke and running mate David Porter outlining their incoming administration - the term that would open that fall. Lyne, who lost the companion 'second spot' race to Porter in the same 1969 election, went on to win the presidency unopposed in March 1970 for 1970-71 (see that year). The plaque's 1970-71 pairing of Lyne and Zielke conflates two separate one-year terms.

  2. Herald 48:30 [31], 22 May 1969 PDF

David Payne 1981-82

What the plaque reads
1982
Where this site places the term
1981-82
Office
President

Plaque reads 1982; this site originally filed him under 1982-83. Board of Regents minutes (30 Jan 1982) and Herald coverage place him as SGA president succeeding Marcel Bush, who resigned 14 Jan 1982, filling the balance of the 1981-82 term. Margaret Ragan, elected president in April 1982, led the separate 1982-83 term - the two are not the same office-year. The Herald of 14 January 1982 photographed Payne, then Associated Student Government vice president, announcing the resignation of president and student regent Marcell Bush, whom he succeeded.

Kayla Shelton 2008-09

What the plaque reads
2009
Where this site places the term
2008-09
Office
President

Plaque plate reads "2009" and had been filed under 2009-10, overlapping Kevin Smiley there; moved to 2008-09. Shelton was executive vice president on Johnathon Boles's Red Towel Party ticket and became president - but not student regent - when Boles resigned on 30 Jan 2009, finishing out his 2008-09 term. Kevin Smiley won the next regular election on 16 April 2009 for the 2009-10 term, so Shelton's presidency did not extend into 2009-10. Confirmed: the Herald of April 2009 describes her as the sitting Student Government Association president, having taken office after Johnathon Boles resigned, and previously senator, student affairs chair, speaker of the senate and executive vice president.

Nicki Taylor 2014-15

What the plaque reads
2014-15
Where this site places the term
2014-15
Office
President

The chambers plaque carries two consecutive plates for this year: "Janet 'Nicki' Seay" and "Nicki Taylor." Herald coverage shows one continuous officeholder, not a succession: she opened the year as "Nicki Seay" (Herald, 28 Aug and 25 Sep 2014), was already being called "Nicki Taylor" by the very next issue on 15 Oct 2014, and was still SGA president under that name through the 2 Apr 2015 election that chose her successor - no election, resignation or hand-off for a second officeholder appears anywhere in that span. Treated here as a single president who changed her surname partway through the term.

Reed Morgan 1968-69

What the plaque reads
1968
Where this site places the term
1968-69
Office
Role unresolved

Single-year plate. Sits between Menser and Straeffer, whose election the Herald reported on 9 May 1968 - so Morgan likely finished a partial term or held the seat between elections. The only Reed Morgan found in the archive is a different, earlier role: chair of the organizing committee that received President Kelly Thompson's approval letter for the Associated Students constitution, 1 April 1966 - two years before this plaque entry. No Herald or WKU Timeline coverage connects a Reed Morgan to a 1968-69 SGA office. Left unresolved rather than moved, since the 1966 role (committee chair, pre-election) is not the same office the plaque implies. Re-checked 4 Aug 2026: fresh TopSCHOLAR searches (name plus SGA/regent/student-government terms) turned up only the same 1963-67 WKU Forensics team roster (M. Reed Morgan, debater) and the 1966 constitution-committee material already cited. Nothing new for 1968-69. The portrait on this page was published in the Herald of 7 April 1966, when Morgan was a graduating senior chairing the committee that drafted the constitution; it predates the plaque's 1968 placement by two years, and no source describes him as president.

Amanda Lich 1999-00

What the plaque reads
1999-2000
Where this site places the term
1999-00
Office
President

Every contemporaneous 1999-2000 source found - the Herald's election and reflection coverage, the SGA's own website, and the organization's 2001 list of former presidents - gives her name as Amanda J. Coates or Amanda Coates, never Lich. A WKU employee working today in the university's Philanthropy & Alumni Engagement office in Bowling Green goes by Amanda Coates Lich; the combined surname is consistent with Coates being a maiden name and Lich a married name, but no source found ties that person directly to the 1999-2000 SGA presidency. The plaque's "Lich" is flagged here, not confirmed. The portrait on this page is captioned Amanda Coates in the Herald of 25 April 2000, in a profile of the sitting Student Government Association president; it is published here as the verified holder of the office, not as confirmation of the plaque's surname.

Spellings the project will not quietly fix

Two names on the plaque are recorded here exactly as they appear on it, because no source has been found that settles them: Hargroave, which may be a misspelling of Hargrove, and Keyanna, which appears elsewhere as Keyana. Guessing at a spelling would put a person in the record under a name they never used.

Telling us

If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear it. Corrections come through the Student Government Association office at Western Kentucky University, and they are recorded on this page with the evidence that settled them.

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
