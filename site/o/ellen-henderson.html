17 September 2024

Fall ballot draws 20 freshman candidates and none for graduate seat

Chief Justice Ellen Henderson reported that 20 students were running for freshman senator, one for Gatton Academy senator, one for international senator and none for graduate senator, with voting set for Sept. 26 and 27 on TopNet. The Senate also passed Resolutions 1-24-F and 2-24-F backing a semester-long fundraising drive to raise $2,000 for Potter Children's Home, beginning with a Jump Air Zone event where 40% of proceeds would go to the fund. Both resolutions were written by sophomore senator Jade Ismail and passed unanimously, committing SGA to four fundraisers, one in September, two in October and one in December, each carrying a QR code pointing students at a GoFundMe. Resolution 2-24-F set out Soarin' in September at Jump Air Zone on Wednesday 25 September from 8 to 10 p.m., a $10 entry covering the two hours and socks, with every participant signing a liability waiver.

30 August 2023

Kurtz seated a five-member Judicial Council and seven committee heads

At SGA's first meeting of the fall semester, the senate approved President Sam Kurtz's nominations of Ashley Ranger, Anna Kate Hardin, Ellen Henderson, Morgan Gammons and Garrison Reed to the 2023-24 Judicial Council. Kurtz said a record number of students had applied and were interviewed over the summer before the field was narrowed to five. He also announced seven committee heads, among them Rush Robinson for Campus Improvements and Sustainability and Karley Solorzano for Diversity, Equity and Inclusion. The new appointees took the oath of office at the meeting.

In the recordHerald, 30 Aug 2023

24 April 2024

24th Senate opens with Vincent as speaker and Henderson as chief justice

Sarah Vincent defeated Sophia Bryant for Speaker of the Senate at the first meeting of the 24th Senate. Anne-Marie Wright was appointed chief of staff and Associate Chief Justice Ellen Henderson was appointed chief justice, all sworn in by outgoing Chief Justice Isaac King. Kurtz announced the Chief Communications Officer post, formerly Director of Public Relations, was open.

In the recordHerald, 24 Apr 2024

29 November 2023

Eight bills create Honors senate seat and set Judicial Council office hours

The final fall meeting passed all eight measures unanimously, including Bill 14-23-F creating a Mahurin Honors College senator seat, sponsored by the Judicial Council and written by Associate Chief Justice Ellen Henderson. Bill 13-23-F raised the chief justice's required office hours from one to five per week and Bill 16-23-F required Judicial Council members to keep two office hours monthly. Leon reported the Senate had spent $1,347.63 of its $9,750 legislative discretionary budget, about 14.87%.

In the recordHerald, 29 Nov 2023

23 April 2024

24th Senate installed: Vincent elected speaker, Henderson named chief justice

At the 24th Senate's first meeting, newly elected senators were sworn in and Sarah Vincent, College of Health and Human Services senator, was elected Speaker of the Senate over Sophia Bryant. Senior senator Anne-Marie Wright was appointed Chief of Staff, and Associate Chief Justice Ellen Henderson was appointed Chief Justice. Outgoing Chief Justice Isaac King swore in the new officers, officially opening the 2024-25 term.

In the recordHerald, 24 Apr 2024

27 August 2024

Executives break down the SGA budget for the Herald editorial board

Taylor told the Herald Editorial Board the 2024-25 budget included $20,000 for organizational aid, $23,500 for scholarships and $10,705 in legislative discretionary funding. Kurtz said the total had stayed at $100,000 for several years and credited Caboni with keeping it untouched. Chief Justice Ellen Henderson said the Senate would vote on election codes at its Sept. 3 meeting and stressed the Judicial Council's independence from the Senate and cabinet.

In the recordHerald, 27 Aug 2024

19 November 2024

Senate funds food pantry and swears in a new chief justice

Bill 17-24-F gave $500 to the Office of Sustainability to restock its food and resource pantry, with Director of Sustainability Leslie North telling senators the office had given away 16,000 items the previous semester. Bill 16-24-F funded 40 Fresh Food Company meal vouchers for the Dean of Students office, and two more bills funded a finals week tabling event and a Boys and Girls Club toy drive. Chief Justice Ellen Henderson swore in Associate Chief Justice Morgan Gammons as chief justice before graduating.

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

What it rests on

Everything here comes from what is publicly available online: the digitised Herald and SGA's own papers on TopSCHOLAR, the Talisman yearbooks, wkuherald.com, the university's news pages and the Wayback Machine. That is a fraction of what happened. Student government has met most weeks for sixty years, and the greater part of it was never written down, or was written down on paper that has never been scanned. A thin year here is a thin record, not a quiet one.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
