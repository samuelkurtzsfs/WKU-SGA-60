16 November 1995

SGA missed its deadline on faculty evaluations

The Herald of 16 November 1995 reported that the Student Government Association would miss its deadline for faculty evaluations, under Charbonee LaBelle's byline. Publishing evaluations had been SGA policy since Resolution 94-1-S and would still be an unfinished project when Amanda Coates revived it in 2000.

In the recordHerald 71:24, 16 Nov 1995 - LaBelle, "Student Government Association to Miss Deadline for Evaluations"

8 April 1999

Jones and Coates square off amid a pizza-party dispute

The Herald's pre-election issue covered the presidential race between Will Jones and Amanda Coates, alongside a report that SGA was questioning an election-eve pizza party Jones had held, a debate over eliminating the rule requiring a year of SGA experience to run for office, and a heated treasurer's race between Ryan Morrison and Joe Matheis.

In the recordHerald 74:49, 8 Apr 1999

13 April 1999Concert

A candidate promises the Dave Matthews Band

Amanda Coates campaigned for the SGA presidency in April 1999 on a pledge to bring a major act to the Hill, with the Dave Matthews Band named in her Herald column. The archive records the promise; it records no booking.

In the recordHerald 74:47, 13 Apr 1999 - Coates, "Dave Matthews Band Could Come to Hill"

15 April 1999

Recount narrows Coates and Martin's win to three votes

Amanda Coates and running mate Cassie Martin initially led Will Jones and Kara Yeckering 616-611 for president and vice president; after the Coates-Martin ticket asked for a recount to be sure of the win, the recount that night found an even closer margin, 614-611. A write-in ticket of Dwight Campbell and Kerri Richardson finished third with 145 votes. Total turnout was 1,436 students.

In the recordHerald 74:51, 15 Apr 1999

15 April 1999

A turbulent election puts Amanda Coates in office

The Herald's coverage of the spring SGA election was headlined "Turbulent Elections Complete"; the same issue carried an opinion piece, "Student Government Association Offers No Leadership," challenging the incoming administration to prove itself.

In the recordHerald 74:51, 15 Apr 1999

26 October 1999Programme

Organizational aid disbursed for Coates's autumn

Bill 99-15-F, dated 26 October 1999, disbursed funds to student organizations. It is the only record of the autumn organizational aid round in Amanda Coates's year.

In the recordTopSCHOLAR - Bill 99-15-F, Disbursement Student Government Association Organizational Aid

22 February 2000

The faculty evaluations campaign opens

President Amanda Coates opened a campaign to publish faculty evaluations as a tool for students choosing professors, starting with a February 22 meeting with the university president and the Faculty Senate chair. The SGA published a step-by-step timeline running through a March 23 presentation to the full Faculty Senate, saying it did not want to win the tool at the expense of unnecessarily upsetting faculty.

In the recordWKU SGA site, Faculty Evaluations (archived 2000)

28 March 2000

Martin and Wright battle to succeed Coates

The Herald reported that Cassie Martin and Duan Wright were competing to succeed Amanda Coates as SGA president in the spring election.

In the recordHerald 75:47, 28 Mar 2000

4 April 2000

Resolution 00-2-S: publish the evaluations

Resolution 00-2-S, authored by Amanda J. Coates and Adam Howard and sponsored by the Legislative Research Committee, asked the Faculty Senate to approve a mandatory SGA-sponsored faculty evaluation to be published beginning in fall 2000. Its attached questionnaire asked practical questions such as whether an instructor takes attendance, offers extra credit or gives comprehensive finals.

In the recordWKU SGA site, Resolution 00-2-S (archived 2000)

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

What it rests on

Everything here comes from what is publicly available online: the digitised Herald and SGA's own papers on TopSCHOLAR, the Talisman yearbooks, wkuherald.com, the university's news pages and the Wayback Machine. That is a fraction of what happened. Student government has met most weeks for sixty years, and the greater part of it was never written down, or was written down on paper that has never been scanned. A thin year here is a thin record, not a quiet one.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
