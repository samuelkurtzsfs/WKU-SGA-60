1975-76
Treasurer

Payne was ASG treasurer in 1975-76. Congress member Marc Levy brought charges against him for spending before budget approval had been given, and Levy appeared twice before the Judicial Council. Payne argued the constitution provided for such spending and the council ruled in his favour. Note for the editor: years.json carries a David Payne under 1982-83 as an unresolved name. These may or may not be the same person and this archive should not merge them without a source that links them.

1976 Talisman, ASG

1976-77
Treasurer

The 1977 Talisman records Payne and Rick Kelley as the two officers beginning second terms in 1976-77. In the ASG supplement published the previous April he had written that the treasurer's job combined record keeping, advising Congress of ASG's financial position, and planning the coming year's budget with the other executive officers. 26 January 1982

David Payne's appeal failed and a regent election was called

Kevin Francke reported that David Payne's appeal had failed and that an election for the student regent seat had been set. Payne had succeeded to the ASG presidency after Marcel Bush's resignation, and the Board of Regents minutes of 30 January 1982 name him as president. Under the previous arrangement the ASG president also sat as student regent, as Steve Fuller had. The seat instead went to a campus-wide vote and Sandra Norfleet won the runoff in February. The Herald index records the appeal and its failure but not the grounds on which it was made.

30 January 1982

Board of Regents minutes name David Payne the new SGA president

Minutes of the WKU Board of Regents meeting identified David Payne as the new Student Government Association president, confirming his succession after Marcel Bush's resignation.

4 February 1982

Payne asks for a second ASG adviser

President David Payne said the Associated Student Government wanted another adviser. The Herald's editorial page criticized his judgment the same week.

12 November 1981

Bush appointed an ASG adviser

Tommy Newton reported that the ASG president had appointed an adviser. The question did not close with the appointment: after Bush resigned, his successor David Payne told the Herald in February that ASG wanted a second one.

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

What it rests on

Everything here comes from what is publicly available online: the digitised Herald and SGA's own papers on TopSCHOLAR, the Talisman yearbooks, wkuherald.com, the university's news pages and the Wayback Machine. That is a fraction of what happened. Student government has met most weeks for sixty years, and the greater part of it was never written down, or was written down on paper that has never been scanned. A thin year here is a thin record, not a quiet one.
