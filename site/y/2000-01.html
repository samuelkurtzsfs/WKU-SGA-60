Cassie Martin president, 2000-01

Office
President
Term recorded here
2000-01
Standing of the record
As recorded on the plaque. Not yet corroborated in the archive.

Confirmed against Herald campaign coverage: Rex Hall's series on the Martin-Wright race ran from the candidacy announcement in late March 2000 through an issues debate on the eve of the 13 April 2000 election.

Cassie Martin, a junior from Glasgow, was elected SGA president in April 2000 to lead the association for 2000-2001. With running mate Leslie Bedo, she defeated the ticket of Duan Wright and Dwight Campbell by 654 votes to 541, and the win also made her the student representative on Western's Board of Regents. The race followed weeks of Herald coverage of proposed SGA election-code reforms meant to close loopholes and set spending limits, and the Martin-Wright contest itself was covered from the candidacy announcement in late March through a pre-election issues debate.

Her most visible stand of the year came at the Board of Regents meeting on October 27, 2000, when the board voted 8-2 to raise the student athletic fee by $80 in two installments. Martin was one of only two dissenters, alongside Faculty Regent Mary Ellen Miller. She said she understood the need for the additional funding but questioned the timing and the amount, and disagreed with raising student fees in the middle of an academic year.

SGA's congress in 2001 passed legislation putting $13,000 toward a campus gazebo that was never built. In October 2002 the Herald reported that the money had mistakenly gone back into SGA's general fund and been spent on other things, including computers, and SGA's finance vice president said leaders from the past two years, including former presidents Martin and Leslie Bedo, were all partly responsible for not properly earmarking it.

The year, in order12

17 February 2000
#

SGA proposed election code rewrite to close loopholes

The Herald reported on new Student Government Association election codes intended to close loopholes in how campaigns were run, ahead of the spring 2000 officer elections that Cassie Martin and Leslie Bedo would go on to win.

College Heights Herald 75:39, 17 Feb 2000

2 March 2000
#

Insurance dispute put SGA "on edge"

The Herald ran a story headlined "Insurance Puts Student Government Association on Edge," in the same issue that covered the ongoing debate over the association's new election codes.

College Heights Herald 75:43, 2 Mar 2000

2 March 2000
#

Election-code debate turned to fairness and spending limits

The Herald reported that SGA's proposed election codes were meant to ensure fairness between candidates and set campaign spending limits, continuing coverage begun two weeks earlier of loopholes in the existing rules.

College Heights Herald 75:43, 2 Mar 2000

28 March 2000
#

Martin and Wright set to battle for SGA presidency

The Herald reported that Cassie Martin and Duan Wright would face off for the SGA presidency in the coming spring election, the first coverage of what became the Martin-Bedo versus Wright-Campbell race.

College Heights Herald 75:47, 28 Mar 2000

13 April 2000
#

Martin and Wright discussed the issues on the eve of the vote

The day before the election, the Herald ran a piece by Rex Hall in which presidential candidates Cassie Martin and Duan Wright discussed the issues facing the campaign.

College Heights Herald 75:52, 13 Apr 2000

14 April 2000
#

Martin-Bedo ticket won SGA presidency

Students elected Cassie Martin, a junior from Glasgow, as SGA president for 2000-2001, a post that also made her the student representative on Western's Board of Regents. Her ticket with vice presidential candidate Leslie Bedo defeated Duan Wright and Dwight Campbell by 654 votes to 541 in the Thursday election. In the only other contested race, Amy Caswell defeated Billy Lyons 706 to 461 for finance vice president, while Adam Howard and Leslie McClard ran unopposed for the administration and public relations vice presidencies.

WKU news release, 14 Apr 2000 (archived)

18 April 2000
#

SGA planned to conduct and publish faculty evaluations

The Herald reported that SGA planned to conduct student evaluations of faculty and publish the results starting in fall 2000, in the same issue that covered the close of the Martin-Bedo campaign. The same issue carried an editorial titled "Cassie Martin Must Stand Up on Tough Issues," urging the incoming president to take firmer positions once in office.

College Heights Herald 75:53, 18 Apr 2000

20 April 2000
#

Herald reported SGA membership still declining

The Herald ran a story headlined "Student Government Association Membership Still Declining," tracking a drop in participation as the Martin administration prepared to take office.

College Heights Herald 75:54, 20 Apr 2000

29 August 2000
#

SGA Congress opened its fall 2000 session

SGA's congress held its first meeting of the fall semester on Tuesday, August 29 at 5 p.m. in Downing University Center room 305. The body met every Tuesday during the semester, and the organization invited students to pick up membership applications in the SGA office on the first floor of DUC.

WKU SGA site, archived 2 Sep 2000

27 October 2000
#

Regents raised athletics fee over Martin's dissent

The Board of Regents voted 8-2 to raise the $16 student athletic fee by $40 in January 2001 and another $40 in fall 2001, to fund a Diddle Arena renovation, a larger football budget for WKU's move to the Gateway Conference, and the athletic department's budget shortfall. Student Regent Cassie Martin and Faculty Regent Mary Ellen Miller cast the two votes against. Martin said she understood the need for the funding but questioned the timing and the amount, and disagreed with raising student fees in the middle of an academic year.

WKU news release, 27 Oct 2000 (archived)

9 March 2001campus
#

WKU took student health service back in-house

WKU announced it would return to self-operation of its campus student health service after its contractor, Collegiate Health Care, notified employees it would be out of business at the end of the day. The university hired the clinic's 17 employees on a temporary basis through the end of the spring semester, and the vice president for Student Affairs promised students would see no reduction in health services.

WKU news release, 9 Mar 2001 (archived)

13 May 2001
#

Bedo ticket stood unopposed to succeed Martin

SGA's candidate page for the 2001 officer elections listed a single ticket for the top job: executive vice president Leslie Bedo for president with Jamie Sears as her running mate. The only race with two names was for vice president of public relations, between Mark Rawlings and Holly Skidmore, while Aaron Spencer and Jamil Sewell stood alone for the finance and administration vice presidencies. The date shown is the Internet Archive capture date; the page itself carried no date and no results or turnout figures were found in allowed sources.

WKU SGA site, archived 13 May 2001

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
