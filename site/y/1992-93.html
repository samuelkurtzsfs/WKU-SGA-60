Joe Rains president and student regent, 1992-93

Office
President and student regent
Term recorded here
1992-93
Standing of the record
As recorded on the plaque. Not yet corroborated in the archive.

First president to serve under the SGA name. Elected 14 April 1992 in a contested race the Herald covered with dueling opinion pieces; took office that autumn once the ASG-to-SGA renaming took effect.

Joe Rains was elected Associated Student Government president on 14 April 1992, a contested race the Herald's election-day issue covered with dueling opinion pieces: an editorial headlined "Associated Student Government Needs Joe Rains" ran alongside a cartoon depicting him as Darth Vader, and the paper carried separate profile pieces on candidates Mark Miller, Mistianna Holcomb and Melony Jones. The same ballot carried the referendum that renamed Associated Students as the Student Government Association; Rains took office that autumn as the first president to serve under the new name.

In office, Rains argued that SGA should support a campus student escort service (Herald, 10 December 1992) and took part in a rally at the state capitol in Frankfort urging the legislature to stop cuts to higher-education funding, for which the WKU University Senate commended him in Resolution R92.11.

The archive does not preserve further detail of how the term ended.

Rains ran for the presidency in April 1992 against Mistianna Holcomb, in the same election week that students voted on a referendum to rename the Associated Student Government as the Student Government Association. The Herald covered both races in a single issue, so his campaign and the rename were decided together.

On election day the Herald's opinion pages carried a letter headlined "Associated Student Government Needs Joe Rains" alongside a Patrick Richardson editorial cartoon depicting Rains as Darth Vader, a sign of how visible the race had become on campus.

The SGA's own list of presidents, published on its website in 2001, records Joe Rains of Nashville, Tennessee as the organization's 26th president, serving 1992-93, and marks him as having also served as student regent.

The digitised record of his year does not name him directly in any indexed description, but it carries one distinctive trace: the minutes for December 1, 1992 record plans for a call-in radio show the association titled 'Just Ask Joe,' alongside Project Western and President for a Day. The same year's minutes show a congress preoccupied with state budget cuts, tuition increases, student health insurance and repeated plans for a rally in Frankfort.

The year, in order16

9 April 1992
#

Herald previews the race and the rename referendum

The Herald previewed the Associated Student Government presidential race under "Mistianna Holcomb, Joe Rains Vying for Presidency," profiled candidate Susan Mitchell under "Susan Mitchell Has a Fresh Perspective," and reported that students would soon vote on a referendum to rename the Associated Student Government the Student Government Association. No source found confirms what office Mitchell sought or whether she won it.

Herald 67:51, 9 Apr 1992

14 April 1992
#

Joe Rains elected SGA president as the ASG-to-SGA renaming took effect

Rains won a contested Associated Student Government presidential election covered by the Herald under the headline "Associated Student Government Needs Joe Rains," alongside a rival editorial cartoon depicting him as Darth Vader and profile pieces on candidates Mark Miller, Mistianna Holcomb and Melony Jones. The same ballot carried the referendum renaming Associated Students as the Student Government Association.

Herald 67:52, 14 Apr 1992

14 April 1992
#

Turnout worries and candidate profiles filled the ballot issue

The same issue that reported Rains' win ran the Herald's concern piece "Many Students Won't Vote" alongside profile pieces on the other candidates: "Mark Miller's Involvement Makes Him a Leader," "Mistianna Holcomb Takes a Stand for Students," and "Melony Jones Loved & Respected by Peers."

Herald 67:52, 14 Apr 1992

25 August 1992
#

Fall opened with a new logo and discount cards

At its first meeting of the fall on August 25, 1992, the student government discussed a new logo, student discount cards, a voter registration drive and committee assignments. The group was still transitioning from the Associated Student Government name to SGA, and its minutes from this year appear under both titles.

SGA Records: Meeting Minutes, 25 Aug 1992

3 September 1992
#

The renamed SGA elects its representatives

In its first weeks under the new name, the Student Government Association held elections for its representatives. The Herald covered the vote at the start of the fall semester.

Herald 68:4, 3 Sep 1992

6 October 1992
#

Support gathers for a rally

The Herald's index for this issue records the new SGA winning support for a campus rally that October, under the headline "Student Government Association Gets Support for Rally."

Herald 68:12, 6 Oct 1992

13 October 1992
#

Resolution asked WKU to spare academics from budget cuts

Resolution 92-7-F asked the university to exempt academic units from a round of proposed budget cuts. State funding pressure ran through the whole fall; the same weeks produced repeated plans for a student rally in Frankfort.

SGA Records: Resolution 92-7-F, 13 Oct 1992

13 October 1992
#

President Meredith addressed the student congress

WKU President Thomas Meredith came to the October 13 meeting to talk about computers, laundry facilities, cable television, lighting and Potter Hall. The same minutes record discussion of budget cuts, homecoming elections, a newsletter and joint forums with the Residence Hall Association.

SGA Records: Meeting Minutes, 13 Oct 1992

20 October 1992
#

Congress honored the Lady Toppers

Resolution 92-8-F honored the WKU women's basketball team for its runner-up finish in the NCAA tournament. It was one of a string of numbered resolutions the congress produced that fall on subjects from drop-add fees to parking.

SGA Records: Resolution 92-8-F, 20 Oct 1992

19 November 1992
#

An invitation to get involved

An opinion piece by Paul Weathers urged students to get involved in the Student Government Association, part of the Herald's running commentary on the body during its first semester under the new name.

Herald 68:23, 19 Nov 1992

December 1992campus
#

Just Ask Joe radio program and Project Western

The December 1 minutes record plans for a Just Ask Joe call-in radio program, the Project Western campus cleanup, a President for a Day event, recycling bins and student health insurance. The meeting closed out a semester dominated by tuition and budget worries.

SGA Records: Meeting Minutes, 1 Dec 1992

10 December 1992
#

Rains pushed SGA to back a student escort service

SGA president Joe Rains argued the organization should support a campus student escort service, the Herald reported.

Herald 68:27, 10 Dec 1992

11 December 1992
#

University Senate honored Rains for a Frankfort budget-cut rally

The WKU University Senate adopted Resolution R92.11, commending SGA president Joe Rains for taking part in a rally at the state capitol in Frankfort asking the legislature to stop cuts to higher-education funding. TopSCHOLAR records the resolution's history running from 11 December 1992 to its adoption on 29 January 1993.

WKU University Senate, Resolution R92.11

2 February 1993campus
#

Meredith returned to pitch Potter Hall as a student service center

President Thomas Meredith addressed the February 2 meeting about campus laundry, cable television and turning Potter Hall into a student service center. Members also discussed tuition increases, resignations and general education requirements.

SGA Records: Meeting Minutes, 2 Feb 1993

13 April 1993campus
#

Spring closed with elections and Earth Day plans

The April 13 meeting took up the budget, spring elections, the annual banquet, a one-card ID system, smoking policy, Adopt-a-Spot and Earth Day. It was the last full meeting recorded in the digitised minutes for the year.

SGA Records: Meeting Minutes, 13 Apr 1993

22 April 1993
#

Donald Smith rolls to victory

The Herald called the spring election with the headline "Student Government Association Election: Donald Smith Rolls to Victory." Smith would succeed Rains for the 1993-94 term.

Herald 68:54, 22 Apr 1993

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
