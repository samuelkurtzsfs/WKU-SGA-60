Rush Robinson
president · plaque: 2025-26
Rush Robinson: Elected 13-15 April 2025 on a turnout of 966. Told the Faculty Senate to 'take a field trip' to see the state of WKU's residence halls. Handed his successor the red suit jacket on 28 April 2026.

What happened, in order

2026-04-07

Two tickets take questions in the DSU auditorium

Caden Lucas with Jakob Barker and Will Derryberry against Jaden Marshall with Kaden Blankenship and Miles VanRude. Chief Justice Sophie Stirling moderated questions submitted by the student body.

Herald, April 2026 ↗
2026-04-15

Turnout jumps 66% in a contested race

1,601 students voted, 635 more than the year before. Lucas won. The clearest evidence in the whole record that WKU turnout tracks whether there is an actual contest.

Herald, April 2026 ↗
2026-04-16

Robinson tells the Faculty Senate to look at the dorms

Sitting next to Faculty Regent Shane Spiller during a discussion of WKU housing, Robinson told senators to 'take a field trip' and see the state of the residence halls.

Herald, April 2026 ↗
2026-04-28

The red suit jacket changes hands

Lucas, Barker and Derryberry sworn in at the first meeting of the 26th Senate. Robinson handed Lucas the same red jacket his own predecessor had given him a year earlier.
