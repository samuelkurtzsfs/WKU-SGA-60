Caden Lucas — the term

Lucas entered SGA in September 2024 as its inaugural student senator for the Mahurin Honors College, a seat created as the college began a program revision; he was also an Honors Teaching Fellow for HON 251, working with first-year Honors students on civic engagement. A junior senator, WKU Spiritmaster, chairman of the Hilltoppers Vote Coalition and an employee of Bowling Green's Downtown Development Division, he won the SGA presidential election on April 15, 2026, with running mates Jakob Barker for vice president and Will Derryberry for chief financial officer. Running under the motto "Lead, build, deliver," his campaign centered on increasing awareness of SGA operations and outreach to the student body, and he defeated Jaden Marshall, who had sought to become the first Black male student body president. After the results were announced Lucas said he felt "electric" and that the "work starts tomorrow," beginning with thank-you cards to supporters.

Lucas took office at the first meeting of the 26th Senate on April 28, 2026, where outgoing president Rush Robinson presented him with the traditional red suit jacket passed from president to president. Lucas thanked the new cabinet, nominated his defeated campaign opponent Jaden Marshall as an at-large senator, calling his campus work "phenomenal," and saw Gabi Pace and Cayden Bailey sworn in as chief of staff and chief communications officer. The meeting's speaker election deadlocked 9-9 between Amelia Tucker and Veronica Butler, a situation the governing documents did not address, and Butler won the seat in a re-vote.

Days after taking office, Lucas was named a 2026 Truman Scholar, WKU's second in the award's history and Kentucky's only recipient that year, chosen from 781 candidates nationally; the scholarship funds $30,000 toward graduate or professional study for students committed to public service. A political science, legal studies and strategic communications major, he had spent that summer establishing the Civic Engagement Resource Center, a joint Mahurin Honors College, SGA and Housing and Residence Life initiative that opened 30 October 2024 with a lending collection on civic life, saying his goal was to get students "talking about what it means to be a good citizen." In May, WKU's Omicron Delta Kappa circle named him its Heather Strode Circle Leader of the Year, an award recognizing collaboration, inclusivity, integrity, scholarship and service.

Before he was sworn in as student regent, Lucas told the Herald he was "generally concerned" by a proposed $210 tuition increase; the Board of Regents approved a $204 increase days later, with outgoing regent Rush Robinson casting the only dissenting vote before handing the seat to Lucas.

What happened, in order

2026-04-08

Executive candidates face off at SGA town hall

SGA held a town hall in the Downing Student Union Auditorium for the two executive tickets: Caden Lucas with Jakob Barker and Will Derryberry, and Jaden Marshall with Kaden Blankenship and Miles VanRude. Chief Justice Sophie Stirling moderated as the candidates answered 27 questions submitted by students. Both tickets made increasing awareness of SGA operations and outreach to the student body their central campaign promise.

Herald, 8 Apr 2026 ↗
2026-04-15

Caden Lucas wins SGA presidential election

Caden Lucas and his ticket of Vice President-elect Jakob Barker and Chief Financial Officer-elect Will Derryberry won the spring election to serve during the 2026-27 academic year, defeating Jaden Marshall's ticket. Lucas, a junior senator, WKU Spiritmaster and chairman of the Hilltoppers Vote Coalition, ran on the motto "Lead, build, deliver" and a promise to make SGA operations more visible to students. The Herald did not publish vote totals or turnout figures; the story also listed the full slate of senators elected to the incoming 26th Senate.

Herald, 15 Apr 2026 ↗
2026-04-24

Lucas named a 2026 Truman Scholar

Days after taking SGA's top office, President Caden Lucas was named a 2026 Truman Scholar, WKU's second in the scholarship's history and Kentucky's only recipient that year. The award, chosen from 781 candidates at 305 colleges nationally, provides $30,000 toward graduate or professional study and recognizes commitment to public service.

WKU News, 24 Apr 2026 ↗
2026-04-28

26th Senate seated; tie vote for speaker resolved in Butler's favor

At the first meeting of the 26th Senate, outgoing president Rush Robinson passed the traditional red suit jacket to Caden Lucas, and the body swore in Gabi Pace as chief of staff, Cayden Bailey as chief communications officer, Maggie Phelps to the judicial council and defeated presidential candidate Jaden Marshall as an at-large senator. The race for speaker of the senate ended in a 9-9 tie between Amelia Tucker and Veronica Butler, and Chief Justice Sophie Sterling confirmed that neither the constitution nor the bylaws addressed a tie. Outgoing speaker Hadley Whipple ordered a re-vote, which elected Butler as speaker.

2026-05-11

Lucas named ODK Circle Leader of the Year

WKU's Omicron Delta Kappa circle named President Caden Lucas its Heather Strode Circle Leader of the Year, an award for a student demonstrating leadership and dedication to the campus community across ODK's five core values.

2026-06-02

Lucas voices concern over proposed tuition increase

WKU released a proposed 2026-27 budget that would raise tuition by $210 to $6,246 per semester within a $405.1 million operating plan. Lucas, identified as SGA president and soon-to-be student regent, said he was "generally concerned" by a proposal that would raise the cost of attending WKU. He said students deserved both a high-quality education and an affordable way to achieve it.

Herald, 2 Jun 2026 ↗
2026-06-05campus

Regents approve tuition increase over student regent's lone no vote

The Board of Regents approved a $204 tuition increase to $6,240 per semester, the sixth consecutive yearly increase, along with a 5% housing rate increase and a 2% meal plan increase as part of a $405 million operating budget. Every regent voted in favor except outgoing SGA president Rush Robinson, casting his final vote as student regent before Lucas took the seat. The board also suspended several majors, including kinesiology and geological sciences, for reworking and mergers.

Herald, 5 Jun 2026 ↗
2026-07-07

SGA got a new adviser in Student Activities leadership change

Andrew Rash, who had served as SGA's faculty adviser and Associate Director of Student Activities and Greek Life since 2022, became interim director of the Department of Student Activities on July 1 following the retirement of longtime director Charley Pride. Assistant Director of Student Activities and Leadership and Volunteerism Amelia Kolb took over as SGA's adviser going forward.
