Leslie Bedo president and student regent, 2001-02

Office
President and student regent
Term recorded here
2001-02
Standing of the record
As recorded on the plaque. Not yet corroborated in the archive.

Appears in the WKU Archives regents index marked as student regent for 2001-02 - useful confirmation that by this point president and student regent were the same person.

Leslie Bedo, a senior corporate communications major from Bowling Green, served as SGA president for 2001-02 after three years in the organization, having previously been parliamentarian, public relations committee chair and executive vice president. Her stated priorities for the year were tuition, student health and online course syllabi, and she described SGA as run "by the students, for the students."

As student regent, Bedo pushed back in August 2001 when the Board of Regents raised tuition 9 percent at a meeting held before fall classes officially began. She said the timing sent "a message to students" that the increase would pass before they could weigh in, though she ultimately joined the board's unanimous vote approving it.

That November, Bedo wrote a Herald op-ed titled "Meeting Goals of Student Government Association, But Not Students'", questioning the organization's effectiveness; fellow member Troy Ransdell responded in the same issue with a piece headlined "Action, Not Complaints." The same paper reported SGA was buying Christmas presents for children at Potter Children's Home that season.

In March 2002, near the end of her term, Bedo publicly challenged the university after a land deal meant to add 400 to 500 parking spaces fell through while the Diddle Arena renovation was removing about 200 spaces. She noted regents had raised the student athletics fee on the expectation of more parking, said "the parking has been put on hold," and called added parking imperative because more students parked on campus than attended basketball games.

As her term wound down, an April 30, 2002 Herald profile by Molly O'Connor reported renewed interest in SGA under Bedo's leadership. Jamie Sears was elected to succeed her that April; Bedo's own former rival for vice president of administration, Patti Johnson, went on to become SGA president herself in a September 2004 special election.

The year, in order13

18 August 2001
#

Bedo questions timing of 9 percent tuition increase, then votes for it

WKU's Board of Regents voted on Friday, August 17 to raise tuition 9 percent starting in fall 2002, the first tuition hike in university history devoted entirely to faculty and staff pay. Student Regent Leslie Bedo said she was troubled that the vote came before students officially began fall classes, telling the board it sent a message that the university would slide the increase through before students could respond. Despite her concerns, Bedo joined the unanimous vote approving the increase, which raised in-state undergraduate tuition $138 a semester, from $1,422 to $1,560.

Daily News, 18 Aug 2001

7 September 2001
#

Regents approve $32.5 million Diddle Arena bond deal funded by student athletics fee

The Board of Regents approved an agreement with the City of Bowling Green to sell up to $32.5 million in bonds to renovate E.A. Diddle Arena, with the university repaying the debt service through its student athletics fee. Planned improvements included air conditioning, new seating, luxury boxes, expanded parking and a pedway from new parking areas to the arena. The renovation was scheduled to run from April to September 2002.

WKU News, 7 Sep 2001

13 September 2001campus
#

Students organize candlelight vigil after September 11 attacks

Two days after the September 11 attacks, WKU held a student-organized candlelight vigil at the Ivan Wilson Fine Arts Center Amphitheater as part of a slate of campus responses. The university also planned a faculty forum at Van Meter Auditorium, opened the Faculty House for reflection and counseling, created a condolences message board to send to New York and Washington, and scheduled a blood drive for September 27.

WKU News, 13 Sep 2001

6 November 2001
#

SGA raises over $800 for Red Cross disaster relief

The SGA homepage, as archived on November 6, 2001, reported that the association had helped raise more than $800 for the Red Cross Disaster Relief Fund following the September 11 attacks. The same page announced that Congress applications were available in DUC 130 and promoted the Campus Spirit Award, which SGA administered that year.

SGA site, archived 6 Nov 2001

9 November 2001
#

Provide-A-Ride carries 929 passengers over twelve fall nights

SGA's Provide-A-Ride service posted its fall 2001 ridership on its website, listing twelve Thursday and Friday nights of operation between September 20 and November 9, 2001. Nightly passenger counts ranged from 49 to 112, for a total of 929 riders over the period.

SGA site, archived 27 Nov 2001

27 November 2001
#

Fall project slate: online syllabi, extended library hours, alcohol policy

SGA's current-projects page, as archived on November 27, 2001, listed the association's work for the semester. Projects included online course syllabi, extended library hours during finals week, a review of the alcohol policy, a 9/11 Remembrance Wall, the SGA Disaster Relief Fund, Provide-A-Ride keychains, the Senior Class Challenge, the Campus Spirit Award, Homecoming Queen 2001 and the Homecoming parade.

SGA site, archived 27 Nov 2001

29 November 2001

SGA organized Christmas presents for Potter Children's Home

The Herald reported that SGA was collecting Christmas presents that fall for children at Potter Children's Home, a Bowling Green residential care facility, in a news piece by staff writer Hollan Holm.

President Bedo and senator Ransdell publicly debated SGA's effectiveness

The same Herald issue ran dueling opinion pieces on SGA: an op-ed by President Leslie Bedo headlined "Meeting Goals of Student Government Association, But Not Students'", followed by a response from fellow member Troy Ransdell titled "Action, Not Complaints." The full text of the exchange is not available online; only the headlines and bylines are indexed.

SGA constitution amended, Spring 2002

WKU SGA Constitution, amended Spring 2002, TopSCHOLAR

27 March 2002
#

Bedo presses parking issue after WKU land purchase collapses

Negotiations for WKU to buy the Forest Park Trailer Park for 400 to 500 new parking spaces broke down over price, even as the Diddle Arena renovation was set to eliminate about 200 spaces permanently. SGA President Leslie Bedo said several regents had voted to increase the student athletics fee on the basis that student parking would grow, and that the promised parking expansion had been put on hold. She argued more students parked on campus than attended basketball games and called more parking imperative.

Daily News, 27 Mar 2002

11 April 2002
#

Jamie Sears elected SGA president for 2002-03

In online voting, junior public relations major Jamie Sears of Melber defeated senior Sam Stinson 1,284 to 594 for SGA president, winning the seat as student representative on the Board of Regents. John Bradley beat Dana Lockhart 1,065 to 730 for executive vice president, Ross Pruitt ran unopposed for vice president of finance with 1,524 votes, Anna Coats edged Holly Skidmore 933 to 809 for vice president of public relations, and Brandon Copeland defeated Patti Johnson 1,106 to 761 for vice president of administration.

WKU News, 11 Apr 2002

26 April 2002
#

Voters approve Supremacy Clause constitutional amendment

SGA's official general election results page, archived on April 26, 2002, recorded that a constitutional amendment titled Supremacy Clause passed in the spring 2002 general election. The page listed the amendment result alongside the vote totals for the five executive races.

SGA site, archived 26 Apr 2002

30 April 2002
#

Herald profile reported renewed interest in SGA as Bedo's term ended

With Bedo's term winding down, the Herald ran a profile by Molly O'Connor headlined "Student Government Association President Sees Renewed Interest in Organization." The full article text is not available online; only the headline and byline are indexed.

College Heights Herald 77:56, 30 Apr 2002

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
