1968-69

Second year of the two-year 1968-70 plate; see 1968-69. Resolved: the 1969 Talisman captions him "Paul Gerard, the Student Regent" and the Herald of 27 February 1969 explains the mechanism - Kentucky's 1968 law required a student regent at every state campus, and because Associated Students president Bill Straeffer was an out-of-state student, the Congress of Associated Students elected Gerard, a Bowling Green junior and junior class president, to the seat instead. He sat as a non-voting member. Contemporary sources print his name without the suffix the plaque carries.

Paul Gerard entered 1969-70 as the sitting student regent, the role in which the Herald had profiled him in February 1969. The student seat still carried no vote.

The limits of the position defined the year around him: in March 1970 the Herald reported the legislature dropping the bill that would have given student regents a vote, under the headline that lawmakers had kept students impotent.

Larry Zielke president, 1969-70

Standing of the record
Moved here from the year the plaque gives. The correction and its evidence are set out below.

Plaque reads 1970-71, paired with John Lyne. Herald coverage places Zielke's actual term here, in 1969-70: he and Frank Genzianelli 'clash for the presidency' in the 25 March 1969 special election edition, and by 22 May 1969 the Herald reports Zielke and running mate David Porter outlining their incoming administration - the term that would open that fall. Lyne, who lost the companion 'second spot' race to Porter in the same 1969 election, went on to win the presidency unopposed in March 1970 for 1970-71 (see that year). The plaque's 1970-71 pairing of Lyne and Zielke conflates two separate one-year terms.

Zielke won the Associated Students presidency in the 25 March 1969 election, beating Frank Genzianelli. David Porter won the companion vice-presidential race the same day, beating John Lyne - the two men who would face each other for the presidency the following year. By 22 May 1969 the Herald was running Zielke and Porter's own outline of their coming administration, in the same issue that covered President Kelly Thompson's resignation announcement.

The plaque records this term one year late, under 1970-71 and paired with Lyne - who did not take office until the following spring. What the Zielke-Porter administration did in office beyond the year-opening outline is not yet confirmed from the archive.

The year, in order13

25 March 1969
#

Genzianelli and Zielke clash for the presidency

The Herald's Associated Students election special edition covered a two-way presidential race between Frank Genzianelli and Larry Zielke, and a separate race for the second officer spot between John Lyne and David Porter.

Herald 48:23, 25 Mar 1969 PDF

22 May 1969
#

Zielke and Porter outline the coming administration

With Larry Zielke elected president and David Porter as his second officer, the Herald ran their plans for the 1969-70 Associated Students administration. The same issue covered President Kelly Thompson's resignation announcement.

Herald 48:30 [31], 22 May 1969 PDF

22 May 1969campus
#

President Kelly Thompson announces his resignation

Thompson, who had approved the Associated Students constitution in 1966, announced he would step down as WKU's president. The Herald covered it in the same issue as the incoming Zielke-Porter student administration.

Herald 48:30 [31], 22 May 1969 PDF

7 October 1969
#

Associated Student Congress endorses the Vietnam Moratorium

The Herald called it a bold step. The clearest early example of the body taking a position outside the campus fence - the same argument that returns in 2017 and 2023.

Herald 49:5, 7 Oct 1969 PDF

4 November 1969campus
#

New constitution sent back for study

The Associated Student Government returned its proposed new constitution for further study while limiting how long the review could run. The same issue reported the group enlisting merchants for a student discount plan.

Herald 49:13, 4 Nov 1969 PDF

7 November 1969
#

Students start grading the faculty

An ASG committee compiled the results of Western's first student teacher-evaluation effort. The Herald had already editorialised demanding the tabulation move faster.

Herald 49:11, 7 Nov 1969 PDF

9 December 1969campus
#

ASG urges a probe of student housing

The Associated Student Government urged an investigation of student housing, one of the fall's most pointed challenges to university policy.

Herald 49:20, 9 Dec 1969 PDF

13 January 1970campus
#

Congress joins the fight against the superboard

The Associated Students Congress joined the fight against a proposed state superboard for higher education, and the Herald's editorial page argued the same week that the student regent deserved a vote.

Herald 49:24, 13 Jan 1970 PDF

10 February 1970
#

'Associate' regents want voting power

Two pieces in one issue: Associated Students seeking voting rights for the student regent, and student and faculty 'associate' regents pressing for a vote. An editorial calls for broad support of Senate Bill 75. Two years in, the seat was still symbolic.

Herald 49:26, 10 Feb 1970 PDF

13 March 1970campus
#

Lyne wins the presidency before the race begins

John Lyne won the Associated Students presidency before the race began, his bid unopposed. The same issue reported the legislature dropping the bill that would have given student regents a vote, under a headline that lawmakers had kept students impotent.

Herald 49:35, 13 Mar 1970 PDF

24 April 1970campus
#

Four constitution changes go to a vote

The Associated Students Congress moved to seek ratification of four constitution changes, and the Herald saluted vice president David Porter as the government's unsung hero.

Herald 49:43, 24 Apr 1970 PDF

8 May 1970campus
#

ASG figures in the Kent State week delegations

During the Strike Western! demonstrations after the Kent State shootings, John Lyne and Larry Zielke were among the students who presented resolutions to President Dero Downing and Dean Charles Keown on May 8, and both sat with the group that received the university's response on May 11.

WKU Timeline: Strike Western!

16 May 1970campus
#

Congress endorses Downing's reply to protesters

As the Strike Western! week wound down, the Associated Student Congress unanimously endorsed President Dero Downing's reply to the protesters, aligning the elected student government with the administration's answer to the demonstrations.

Herald 49:49, 16 May 1970

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
