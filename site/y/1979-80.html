Standing of the record
Confirmed in the sources below.

Surname spelling on the plaque is HARGROAVE. The Herald, in a 30 August 1979 profile calling him ASG Head at the start of the year, spells the name 'Jamie Hargrove.' Flagging the discrepancy rather than silently correcting the plaque spelling, per project rule.

By August 30, 1979, at the start of the school year, the Herald profiled the Associated Student Government head - spelled 'Jamie Hargrove' in the byline - under the headline that he believed students were interested. The archive does not yet hold the spring 1979 election coverage that put him in office; see the events below for the primary that preceded it.

The archive does not yet show what Hargrove's administration passed or fought over during 1979-80 beyond the record's general sweep of the year. Further Herald issues from the fall and spring semester have not yet been checked.

The year, in order13

12 April 1979
#

830 students vote in the ASG primary; the Herald runs a defence of not voting

The Associated Student Government's spring primary drew 830 voters and put the open-house policy on the ballot. The same issue carried a piece justifying student apathy in ASG voting - the paper had moved from scolding non-voters to explaining them. This primary elected the officers who took office for 1979-80; it was previously misfiled under 1978-79.

Herald 54:54, 12 Apr 1979

August 1979campus
#

Donald Zacharias sworn in as WKU's sixth president

Zacharias, previously executive assistant to the chancellor of the University of Texas system, took office as WKU's sixth president at the opening of the academic year; he was formally installed the following spring.

WKU Timeline: Donald Zacharias

30 August 1979
#

ASG head Jamie Hargrove profiled at year's start

The Herald profiled the Associated Student Government head under the headline that he believed students were interested, in the same issue that introduced the new president's wife, Tommie Zacharias, to campus.

Herald 55:2, 30 Aug 1979

30 August 1979campus
#

Hargrove opens the year saying students are interested

Associated Student Government head Jamie Hargrove told the Herald he believed students were interested in their government as the year opened. A week later ASG heard its 1979-80 budget and discussed changing its delegate policy.

Herald 55:2, 30 Aug 1979

13 September 1979campus
#

ASG weighs expanding hall representation

The Associated Student Government considered expanding residence hall representation in its congress, in the same issue reporting the Board of Regents set to consider a two-year budget request.

Herald 55:5, 13 Sep 1979

20 September 1979campus
#

Seats bill passes after revision under pressure

A bill on Associated Student Government seats passed after being revised under pressure, expanding on earlier talk of broader residence hall representation. The Herald called the representation bill a small step in the right direction.

Herald 55:7, 20 Sep 1979

November 1979campus
#

ASG rejects move to close Diddle Hall

The Associated Student Government rejected a move to close Diddle Hall dormitory. The vote came amid a fall of housing debates that later included a push to make Keen Hall a coed dorm.

Herald 55:19, 1 Nov 1979

13 November 1979
#

Resolution 79-10 seeks staff for scholarship administration

The Associated Student Government passed Resolution 79-10, urging the university to assign staff to administer academic scholarships.

SGA Resolution 79-10, Academic Scholarship Expansion

29 November 1979campus
#

ASG proposes an activity fee for part-time students

ASG proposed a part-time student activity fee. The proposal came as the Board of Regents weighed budget requests for the coming biennium.

Herald 55:25, 29 Nov 1979

21 February 1980campus
#

ASG considers moving its spring elections

The Associated Student Government considered changing the date of its spring elections. The discussion took place against campus anger over a proposed tuition increase.

Herald 55:40, 21 Feb 1980

17 April 1980campus
#

Fuller and Irons win the presidential primary

Steve Fuller and Tim Irons emerged as victors in the ASG presidential primary, from a field of three that also included David Rue. Candidates had been certified earlier in the month.

Herald 55:53, 17 Apr 1980

24 April 1980campus
#

Steve Fuller elected ASG president

Steve Fuller, Mark Wilson and Greg Zoeller were elected to the top Associated Student Government jobs. The Herald wrote that persistence had paid off in Fuller's campaign, while runner-up Tim Irons looked to the future after losing.

Herald 55:55, 24 Apr 1980

May 1980campus
#

New ASG officers and members sworn in

The new Associated Student Government officers and members were sworn in. ASG and the Interhall Council were also conducting student polls as the year closed.

Herald 55:57, 1 May 1980

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
