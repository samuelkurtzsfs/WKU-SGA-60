Steve Henry president, 1975-76

Office
President
Term recorded here
1975-76
Standing of the record
Confirmed in the sources below.

Likely the Steve Henry who later served as Lieutenant Governor of Kentucky (1995-2003). Wikipedia's biography of Stephen L. Henry (b. 8 Oct 1953) states he was elected WKU Student Body President "in his second attempt, after losing a close election the preceding year to Jeff Costello," and served on the WKU Board of Regents as a Kentucky resident while president - matching this record's own Herald sourcing almost exactly: Jeffrey Consolo (not "Costello", likely a Wikipedia transcription of the same name) won the presidency in spring 1974, and the Herald's 30 Apr 1976 wrap-up of Henry's term specifically describes him balancing the dual jobs of student regent and ASG president. The match in name, university, era, and this specific narrative beat is treated as verification, but the identification rests on a secondary source (Wikipedia), not a WKU archive document that states it outright - flagged here rather than treated as fully closed.

Steve Henry appeared in ASG coverage as early as September 1972, when the Herald reported his view that the Associated Student Government had lost respect.

Henry won the spring 1974 election on a ticket with Jeff Consolo, and resigned his post running the student discount program after the victory.

As president in 1975-76 Henry moved in November to change ASG's concert proposal after a money-losing fall season, while a student group urged support for his energy awareness plan. At year's end the Herald summed up his tenure with a piece on how the role had required balancing the twin jobs of student regent and ASG president, which he held at the same time.

A Wikipedia biography of Stephen L. Henry, later the 52nd Lieutenant Governor of Kentucky (1995-2003), describes an orthopedic surgeon and WKU alumnus who was elected Student Body President on his second attempt after an earlier loss, and who sat on the WKU Board of Regents as president - a career arc that lines up point for point with this record's own Herald sourcing, though no WKU archive document has yet been found that states the identification directly.

26 March 1976campus
#

Faulk impeachment hearings open as campaign begins

The Associated Student Government began impeachment hearings against congress member Gerard Faulk the same week candidates for the spring election, including Christy Vogt, were set to speak to students. An editorial the same issue held up Associated Student Government's troubles as a cautionary example for the university's newly forming Faculty Senate.

9 April 1976campus
#

Christy Vogt wins the ASG presidency

The Herald reported Christy Vogt's win in the Associated Student Government presidential election under the headline "Christy Vogt Wins Election," with Vogt's own "Winner Says Thanks" running in the same issue. The paper reported the result the same week Gerard Faulk was acquitted in his impeachment case.

Herald 51:53, 9 Apr 1976 PDF

27 April 1976campus
#

Herald supplement recaps a year of ASG wins

A special supplement to the Herald, paid for and written by the Associated Student Government, summed up the outgoing Henry administration's year: women's hours abolished with ASG's backing, better intramural facilities credited to ASG resolutions, a new ASG discount card program, a second campus site for cashing checks, and a Veterans on Campus book exchange. The supplement also printed the year's roster of congress members and its bills and resolutions, and carried Christy Vogt's own piece on laundry facility improvement.

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
