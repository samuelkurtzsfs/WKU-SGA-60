William Menser president and student regent, 1967-68

Office
President and student regent
Term recorded here
1967-68
Place in the line
the 2nd president and the 1st student regent
Standing of the record
Confirmed in the sources below.

ASG president 1967-68. On 4 April 1968 the Herald reported he 'assumes duties as Regent Board Member' - making him WKU's first student regent, two months after the enabling bill was reported in committee. The seat was non-voting at first.

Menser headed the Associated Student Government from the start of the 1967-68 year, appearing alongside President Kelly Thompson in a 14 September 1967 Herald article welcoming students back to campus.

That fall the idea of a student seat on the Board of Regents was already circulating: a 2 November 1967 Herald article headlined 'Ward Favors Student on Board of Regents' reported support for the idea four months before an enabling bill reached committee.

That December the Herald reported an Associated Students survey of student activity preferences and, in the same 8 December 1967 issue, ran a piece by Dennis Jaffe commending the government's efforts; the same issue covered the Kentucky Intercollegiate Press Association backing a proposal for student representation.

In February 1968 Western sent lobbyists to Frankfort for a bill to seat a student on the Board of Regents; on 4 April 1968 the Herald reported Menser had assumed duties as the board's first student regent, a non-voting seat.

As his term closed, the Herald's 29 April 1968 issue - the same one covering the Straeffer-Whitley presidential race to succeed him - carried both a piece headlined 'Menser Discusses Head Fee' and a separate retrospective, 'Past Performance of Associated Student Government Analyzed'. The archive does not yet hold the text of either article, only their headlines.

Menser opened his year in office in September 1967 sharing the front page with President Kelly Thompson, in a piece headlined "Dr. Kelly Thompson, William Menser Welcome Students." The same first issue of the year set out the Associated Student Government's schedule of events.

Menser's correspondence with President Kelly Thompson survives in the WKU Archives, filed under student fees. The file is described as correspondence about the assessment of student fees and the Associated Student Government budget for 1968-1969.

#

Menser and President Thompson open the school year together

The first Herald of the 1967-68 year carried a joint welcome to students headlined "Dr. Kelly Thompson, William Menser Welcome Students." The same issue ran an item headed "Associated Student Government Events Set," laying out the organisation's calendar for the coming term.

The Associated Student Government sets its events for the year

The first Herald of 1967-68 announced the Associated Student Government's schedule of events for the coming year alongside the university's own lecture series and the Community Concerts season. It was the first year the government published a programme in advance.

The Herald says the praise for student government is deserved

A Herald editorial headed "Praise for Associated Student Government Well Deserved" ran in November 1967, an unusually warm note in a paper that spent most of the era complaining about what the government booked.

Herald 47:12, 16 Nov 1967 PDF

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you: people who held an office can be given the keys to their own year on the contribute page.

What it rests on

Everything here comes from what is publicly available online: the digitised Herald and SGA's own papers on TopSCHOLAR, the Talisman yearbooks, wkuherald.com, the university's news pages and the Wayback Machine. That is a fraction of what happened. Student government has met most weeks for sixty years, and the greater part of it was never written down, or was written down on paper that has never been scanned. A thin year here is a thin record, not a quiet one.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
