William Menser president, 1967-68

Term recorded here
1967-68
Standing of the record
Confirmed in the sources below.

ASG president 1967-68. On 4 April 1968 the Herald reported he 'assumes duties as Regent Board Member' - making him WKU's first student regent, two months after the enabling bill was reported in committee. The seat was non-voting at first.

Menser headed the Associated Student Government from the start of the 1967-68 year, appearing alongside President Kelly Thompson in a 14 September 1967 Herald article welcoming students back to campus.

That fall the idea of a student seat on the Board of Regents was already circulating: a 2 November 1967 Herald article headlined 'Ward Favors Student on Board of Regents' reported support for the idea four months before an enabling bill reached committee.

That December the Herald reported an Associated Students survey of student activity preferences and, in the same 8 December 1967 issue, ran a piece by Dennis Jaffe commending the government's efforts; the same issue covered the Kentucky Intercollegiate Press Association backing a proposal for student representation.

In February 1968 Western sent lobbyists to Frankfort for a bill to seat a student on the Board of Regents; on 4 April 1968 the Herald reported Menser had assumed duties as the board's first student regent, a non-voting seat.

As his term closed, the Herald's 29 April 1968 issue - the same one covering the Straeffer-Whitley presidential race to succeed him - carried both a piece headlined 'Menser Discusses Head Fee' and a separate retrospective, 'Past Performance of Associated Student Government Analyzed'. The archive does not yet hold the text of either article, only their headlines.

Menser opened his year in office in September 1967 sharing the front page with President Kelly Thompson, in a piece headlined "Dr. Kelly Thompson, William Menser Welcome Students." The same first issue of the year set out the Associated Student Government's schedule of events.

Menser's correspondence with President Kelly Thompson survives in the WKU Archives, filed under student fees. The file is described as correspondence about the assessment of student fees and the Associated Student Government budget for 1968-1969.

The year, in order18

14 September 1967
#

Menser and President Thompson open the school year together

The first Herald of the 1967-68 year carried a joint welcome to students headlined "Dr. Kelly Thompson, William Menser Welcome Students." The same issue ran an item headed "Associated Student Government Events Set," laying out the organisation's calendar for the coming term.

Herald 47:1, 14 Sep 1967 PDF

14 October 1967campus
#

Rodes-Harlin Hall opens as a nine-story women's dorm

The Herald covered the opening of Rodes-Harlin Hall, a new nine-story women's residence hall, as campus construction continued to expand housing capacity.

Herald 47:6, 14 Oct 1967 PDF

2 November 1967
#

Herald floats a student seat on the Board of Regents

A Herald article headlined 'Ward Favors Student on Board of Regents' reported support for putting a student on the Board of Regents, months before Western sent lobbyists to Frankfort or a bill reached committee.

Herald 47:10, 2 Nov 1967 PDF

2 November 1967
#

ASG sponsors a Friday dance

The same issue that floated a student regent seat also reported the Associated Student Government sponsoring a campus dance that Friday.

Herald 47:10, 2 Nov 1967 PDF

8 December 1967
#

ASG survey of student activity preferences

The Associated Students published a survey of what activities students wanted, reported by Ellen Bennett in the same issue as a Herald piece commending the government's efforts and coverage of the Kentucky Intercollegiate Press Association backing a proposal for student representation.

Herald 47:14, 8 Dec 1967 PDF

8 December 1967
#

Kentucky Intercollegiate Press Association backs student representation

The statewide student press association came out for a proposal for student representation, reported in the same Herald issue as the Associated Students' activity survey.

8 December 1967
#

Associated Students survey students on activity preferences

The Herald reported the results of an Associated Students survey under the headline "Associated Students' Survey Reveals Activity Preferences." The same issue carried a letter headed "Commends Associated Student Government's Efforts."

Herald 47:14, 8 Dec 1967 PDF

8 December 1967
#

State press association backs student representation proposal

The 8 December 1967 Herald reported that the Kentucky Intercollegiate Press Association had come out in support of the proposal for student representation. The item ran alongside the paper's coverage of Associated Students activity that week.

Herald 47:14, 8 Dec 1967 PDF

8 February 1968
#

A bill to put a student on the Board of Regents reaches committee

Reported by Ron Lawrence. Western had sent lobbyists to Frankfort for it earlier that month. The same issue carries students berating dorm rules and a letter headed 'Wanted: Work for Judicial Council'.

Herald 47:18, 8 Feb 1968

22 February 1968
#

Herald questions ASG's Judicial Council as students organize a book swap

Bill Martin's column questioned the Judicial Council's work, while the same issue reported the Associated Student Government directing a student book swap.

Herald 47:20, 22 Feb 1968

29 February 1968
#

WKU sends lobbyists to Frankfort for a student on the Board of Regents

The same Herald issue carries a tuition increase, a discussion group on major issues before the Associated Student Government and a committee investigating women's dormitory rules. This is the origin of the student regent seat.

Herald 47:20 [21], 29 Feb 1968

29 February 1968
#

Associated Students discussion group takes up major campus issues

The Herald announced that major issues would be heard in an Associated Student Government discussion group. The same issue reported that Western had sent lobbyists in support of a bill to place a student on the Board of Regents, and carried coverage of a tuition increase.

Herald 47:20 [21], 29 Feb 1968

4 April 1968
#

William Menser becomes WKU's first student regent

Two months after the bill was in committee, the Herald reported that Menser 'assumes duties as Regent Board Member'. The seat carried no vote at first. The same issue opens candidate declarations for the next student government election.

Herald 47:26, 4 Apr 1968

11 April 1968
#

Filing reveals few candidates for the spring election

Alana White reported that filing for Associated Student Government offices turned up few candidates, in the same issue as a piece urging that criteria for the offices be voiced.

Herald 47:27, 11 Apr 1968

29 April 1968
#

Menser discusses the head fee

The same issue that opened coverage of the Straeffer-Whitley race to succeed him carried a piece on outgoing president Menser discussing the student head fee. The archive does not yet hold the article's text.

Herald 47:28, 29 Apr 1968

29 April 1968
#

Herald analyzes ASG's past performance under Menser

A retrospective headlined 'Past Performance of Associated Student Government Analyzed' ran as Menser's term ended, in the same issue as the Straeffer-Whitley presidential campaign coverage. The archive does not yet hold the article's text.

Herald 47:28, 29 Apr 1968

29 April 1968
#

Herald reviews the Associated Student Government's record

As Menser's term drew to a close the Herald ran an assessment headed "Past Performance of Associated Student Government Analyzed." It appeared in the same issue as the campaign coverage for the officers who would succeed him.

Herald 47:28, 29 Apr 1968

9 May 1968
#

Bill Straeffer wins the presidency; the Herald calls the election a failure

The issue reporting Straeffer's win also carries an editorial headed 'Elections Illustrate Faults, Indifference'. Turnout anxiety shows up in the second contested election the organisation ever held.

Herald 47:30, 9 May 1968

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
