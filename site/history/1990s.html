1990-91

Michael Colvin (president)

23 entries · 66 index lines

19 April 1990

Michael Colvin elected Associated Student Government president

Colvin won the Associated Student Government's presidential election, the Herald reported under the headline "Michael Colvin Aces Associated Student Government Election."

ASG bill would put microwaves in cafeterias

In the recordSGA Legislation, Bill #90-4-F, "Microwaves"

13 September 1990

Glasgow's student body budget cut

ASG cut the budget of the WKU Glasgow associated student body. The move came a year after the main campus organization had helped Glasgow change its constitution.

In the recordHerald 66:7, 13 Sep 1990

29 November 1990

ASG passes resolution for left-handed desks

The Associated Student Government passed Resolution 90-10-F, requesting that a left-handed desk be provided in each classroom. The Herald reported the vote in its 29 November 1990 issue, alongside news that a companion resolution on excused absences had been killed the same week.

In the recordSGA Legislation, Resolution 90-10-F, "Left Handed Desks"

29 November 1990

ASG kills resolution on earthquake-prediction absences

The Associated Student Government killed Resolution 90-12-F, which had asked that student absences tied to Iben Browning's widely publicized forecast of a major New Madrid Fault earthquake be excused. Browning had predicted the quake for on or near 3 December 1990; it prompted regional anxiety and some school closures, but no earthquake occurred. The Herald reported the resolution's defeat in its 29 November 1990 issue.

In the recordSGA Legislation, Resolution 90-12-F, "Excused Absences for Earthquake Prediction"

29 November 1990

Left-handed desks pushed; excused absences killed

ASG pushed for left-handed desks in classrooms and killed a resolution on excused absences in the same stretch of meetings. The two items closed out the fall legislative calendar.

In the recordHerald 66:27, 29 Nov 1990

1991

ASG votes to join the Hilltopper Athletic Foundation

The Associated Student Government passed Bill #91-3-S, providing for ASG membership in the Hilltopper Athletic Foundation.

In the recordSGA Legislation, Bill #91-3-S, "ASG to Join Hilltopper Athletic Foundation"

1991

ASG overhauls its by-laws, including veto-override procedure

In the spring term the Associated Student Government passed a cluster of by-law bills: Bill #91-4-S suspended the by-laws to allow a vote on a pending matter, Bill #91-5-S amended the by-laws on how resolutions could be submitted, and Bill #91-6-S changed the by-laws on overriding a presidential veto. The new override procedure was tested weeks later when the Senate's emergency-kit resolution survived a veto on 14 March 1991.

In the recordSGA Legislation, Bill #91-6-S, "By-Laws"

ASG resolves to improve Detrex Field

The Associated Student Government passed Resolution 91-7-S, requesting improvements to Detrex Field, the campus intramural field.

ASG bill would recognize presidential scholars in the Herald

The Associated Student Government introduced Bill #91-7-S, a bill separate from the same-numbered resolution on Detrex Field, proposing that WKU's presidential scholars be recognized in the College Heights Herald.

An ASG survey supported creating a dead week before finals. The same issue carried an opinion piece headlined that Colvin failed on final examinations.

The Herald reported that a change in insurance practice was "upsetting" the Associated Student Government; the archive does not preserve further detail of what the change was or how ASG responded.

Students held a campus vigil as the Gulf War approached

The same Herald issue covered a campus prayer vigil for peace as the U.N. deadline for Iraq's withdrawal from Kuwait neared, two days before the United States led a coalition into the Gulf War.

In the recordHerald 66:32, 15 Jan 1991, "Students Pray for Peace"

Two officers step down

Amos Gott and Van Hodge stepped down as ASG officers, and the remaining officers clashed over comprehensive exams in the same issue's coverage. Two weeks later Karl Miller was picked as public relations vice president.

The Associated Student Government considered the idea of moving to a four-day class week, the Herald reported.

Tuition caps, Detrex, and financial aid

In a single February issue, ASG wanted to cap tuition increases, proposed an overhaul of Detrex registration, and planned lobbying to protect financial aid. It was the broadest policy week of the Colvin year.

In the recordHerald 66:40, 12 Feb 1991

14 March 1991

Emergency kit resolution survives a veto

A resolution on emergency kits survived a veto and stood as passed. It was the only veto fight reported in the Herald index that year.

In the recordHerald 66:49, 14 Mar 1991

Heather Falmlen wins amid protests

Heather Falmlen won the ASG presidency amid election protests, with a group urging students to boycott the vote and an editorial condemning immature antics in the campaign. The results issue reported three races as clear-cut.

25 entries · 56 index lines

2 April 1991

Herald previews four ASG candidates

The Herald's "Meet the Candidates" feature previewed four contenders in the spring Associated Student Government elections: Heather Falmlen, Rob Evans, Van Hodge, and Dan Knowles. Falmlen went on to win the race.

In the recordHerald 66:51, 2 Apr 1991, "Meet the Candidates"

Heather Falmlen wins the ASG presidency amid election protests

Falmlen won the Associated Student Government presidential election in results the Herald reported under the headline "Heather Falmlen Wins Amid Associated Student Government Election Protests." The same issue covered a rival group's call to boycott the election, an editorial calling the contest "immature antics," and a complaint that the Herald's own coverage had been biased.

WKU's own "On Campus" newsletter reported Heather Falmlen as both Student Regent and Associated Student Government President, a contemporaneous confirmation that the two offices - separately elected two decades earlier - were held by the same person entering her term.

ASG asks for a change in Big Red Shuttle fares

29 October 1991

ASG asks that Iracane not be re-elected regents chair

The Associated Student Government passed Resolution 91-6-F, authored by Mark Miller and Eric McWilliams and sponsored by Mistianna Holcomb, asking that Joe Iracane not be re-elected chair of the Board of Regents. The resolution's text stated that Iracane, chairman for eight consecutive years, was under investigation by the FBI, the IRS and U.S. attorneys in Indiana and Kentucky over an alleged $6 million payment to Pyramid Mining Co. in return for contracts and a separate alleged $14,500 payment to the company for a consulting contract; it also accused him of overstepping into day-to-day university operations reserved for the president under the Board's bylaws. The resolution asked student regent Heather Falmlen to vote against him. No source found reports the investigation's outcome. The same day's Herald covered the vote under "Associated Student Government Opposes Joseph Iracane."

In the recordSGA Resolution 91-6-F, "Board of Regents Chair Term Limit"

31 October 1991

ASG asks the regents to elect a new leader

ASG urged the Board of Regents to elect a new leader in place of chairman Joseph Iracane, two days after the Herald reported the organization's opposition to him. It was a rare direct challenge to the university's governing board.

In the recordHerald 67:19, 31 Oct 1991

6 November 1991campus

Regents re-elect Iracane despite ASG's resolution

A week after the Associated Student Government asked the Board of Regents not to re-elect Joe Iracane as chair, the board did so anyway. The university's own newsletter reported the result under "Joe Iracane Re-elected to Chair Regents."

In the recordOn Campus 1:16, 6 Nov 1991, "Joe Iracane Re-elected to Chair Regents" PDF

28 January 1992

ASG introduces a bill for a student regent advisory committee

The Associated Student Government introduced Bill #92-01-S, authored by Greg Butler, Mistianna Holcomb, Melony Jones and Melissa Maggard and sponsored by the Public Relations committee, proposing a Student Advisory Committee - made up of the class presidents, a representative from each college and the executive council as ex-officio members - to brief the student regent on Board of Regents agenda items and canvass student opinion before each meeting, two days before the Herald reported the plan.

In the recordSGA Legislation, Bill #92-01-S, "Development of Advisory Committee for Student Regent"

ASG plans a committee on student access to the regent

The Herald reported the Associated Student Government was planning a committee aimed at securing student access to WKU's regent, amid a statewide debate over how regents were selected.

In the recordHerald 67:34, 30 Jan 1992, "Associated Student Government Plans Regent Committee"

30 January 1992

The same Herald issue ran an editorial cartoon, "Ventriloquist's Dummy" by Patrick Richardson, with Falmlen as its subject.

In the recordHerald 67:34, 30 Jan 1992, "Editorial Cartoon Heather Falmlen Ventriloquist's Dummy"

2 April 1992

ASG resolves to support football despite budget cuts

The Associated Student Government passed Resolution 92-7-S, declaring its support for the WKU football program in spite of budget cuts. The resolution's own text argued the university had already awarded 67 football scholarships it would be contractually bound to honor even if the program were suspended, calling a suspension that still paid non-playing scholarship athletes an unnecessary expense. This entry corrects the resolution's date: an earlier record in this archive gave 4 February 1992, but the resolution itself is dated introduced 2 April 1992, shortly before the Board of Regents' 1 May vote to keep the program.

In the recordSGA Legislation, Resolution 92-7-S, "Support of Football Program"

7 April 1992

Associated Students votes to become the Student Government Association

The body voted to rename itself on 7 April and students ratified the change in a referendum on 14 April. It took effect that autumn.

In the recordWKU SGA history

The primary is canceled

The ASG primary election was canceled, with too few candidates to require one, and a parking bill sat in committee the same week. The general election went ahead the following Tuesday.

In the recordHerald 67:50, 7 Apr 1992

16 April 1992

Joe Rains wins big as 1,200 turn out

Joe Rains won the presidential election by a wide margin, with about 1,200 students casting votes. The same ballot carried the constitutional amendments students had voted on two days earlier, including the name change.

In the recordHerald 67:53, 16 Apr 1992

21 April 1992

Rains to oversee the change to SGA

The Herald reported that incoming president Joe Rains would oversee the transition from the Associated Student Government to the Student Government Association. Student reaction to the spring's parking bill was reported as varied in the same issue.

In the recordHerald 67:54, 21 Apr 1992

May 1992campus

Board of Regents votes to keep football

The Board of Regents voted to keep the football program, following months of budget pressure that had prompted the ASG's February resolution of support.

Matheis survives a flier scandal to win vice president of finance

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you through the Student Government Association office.

Reuse

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
