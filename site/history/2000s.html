Martin-Bedo ticket won SGA presidency

27 October 2000

Regents raised athletics fee over Martin's dissent

The Board of Regents voted 8-2 to raise the $16 student athletic fee by $40 in January 2001 and another $40 in fall 2001, to fund a Diddle Arena renovation, a larger football budget for WKU's move to the Gateway Conference, and the athletic department's budget shortfall. 18 August 2001

Bedo questions timing of 9 percent tuition increase, then votes for it

WKU's Board of Regents voted on Friday, August 17 to raise tuition 9 percent starting in fall 2002, the first tuition hike in university history devoted entirely to faculty and staff pay. Student Regent Leslie Bedo said she was troubled that the vote came before students officially began fall classes, telling the board it sent a message that the university would slide the increase through before students could respond. Despite her concerns, Bedo joined the unanimous vote approving the increase, which raised in-state undergraduate tuition $138 a semester, from $1,422 to $1,560.

Regents approve $32.5 million Diddle Arena bond deal funded by student athletics fee

The Board of Regents approved an agreement with the City of Bowling Green to sell up to $32.5 million in bonds to renovate E.A. Planned improvements included air conditioning, new seating, luxury boxes, expanded parking and a pedway from new parking areas to the arena. The renovation was scheduled to run from April to September 2002.

What this is

A year-by-year record of student government at Western Kentucky University, from the ratification of the Associated Students constitution in April 1966 to the present. Every entry names the source it came from.

Corrections

The names here start with the plaque in the SGA Chambers, and the plaque is known to be wrong in places. Where the archive disagrees with it, this site follows the archive and records the change on the corrections page. If you served, or you know that a year here is wrong, the project wants to hear from you: people who held an office can be given the keys to their own year on the contribute page.

What it rests on

Everything here comes from what is publicly available online: the digitised Herald and SGA's own papers on TopSCHOLAR, the Talisman yearbooks, wkuherald.com, the university's news pages and the Wayback Machine. That is a fraction of what happened. Student government has met most weeks for sixty years, and the greater part of it was never written down, or was written down on paper that has never been scanned. A thin year here is a thin record, not a quiet one.

Text on this site is the work of the project. Photographs, documents and legislation are reproduced from Western Kentucky University's own open archives and from the College Heights Herald and the Talisman; each item names the collection, issue and page it came from and links to the original.
