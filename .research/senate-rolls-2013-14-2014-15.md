# Senate rolls: 2013-14 and 2014-15, 22 August 2026

Source: SGA's own Senate minutes, hosted as Word documents under
wku.edu/sga/uploads/minutes/. Fetched via `wku.edu/sga/legislative/minutes.php`,
which links every individual minutes file back to 2009-10 with a plain `<a href>`.

## A dating trap worth recording for future passes

wku.edu's `/uploads/minutes/<year>/` folders are filed by the year the
**academic year started**, not the calendar year of each individual meeting.
The `/2013/` folder holds meetings from August-December 2013 (fall 2013-14)
*and* January-May 2013 (spring, actually 2012-13) *and*, in two files
(`minutes11-27.docx`, `minutes12-4.docx`), meetings dated November-December
**2012** under the eleventh Senate and President Cory Dodds -- a full year
off from what the folder name implies.

The `/2014/` folder is worse: of the nineteen files with January-May
filenames that looked like 2013-14 spring meetings at first glance, only
seven actually are (President Keyana Boka, bill suffix `-14-S`). The other
twelve -- filenames `1-27`, `2-3`, `2-10`, `2-241`, `3-3`, `3-17`, `3-24`,
`3-31`, `4-7`, `4-14`, `4-21`, `5-5` -- are spring **2015** meetings of the
Thirteenth Senate under President Nicki Taylor (bill suffix `-15-S`), simply
filed a year late by the site's own folder convention. A future pass should
not trust a filename or folder year for this corpus without opening the
document and checking the President's name, the Senate's ordinal number, and
the bill-suffix year against what years.json already has on record --
several of the wrong-year files would have misattributed Nicki Taylor's own
senate to 2013-14 if merged on filename alone.

## 2013-14 (12th Senate, President Keyana Boka)

Went from 10 recorded members to 21. Added, all confirmed against the primary
minutes text by a separate adversarial verifier before merging:

Hayley Wilkins, Tyler Jenkins and Bradley Blankenship -- three senators
already resigned by the term's first meeting, 27 August 2013. Natalie
Broderick -- Student Affairs committee chair, resigned by 3 September 2013.
Chris Mckenna -- Academic Affairs vice head, appointed 29 October 2013.
Alexis Baker -- Campus Improvements chair, appointed 29 October 2013,
resigned by 25 March 2014. Cole McDowell -- senator, appointed 11 February
2014, then Secretary of the Senate the same night. Jay Todd Richey -- chair,
Legislative Research Committee, appointed 11 February 2014; on 15 April 2014
the outgoing 12th Senate elected him to serve as Speaker of the incoming
13th Senate for 2014-15 (not a 2013-14 speakership -- Paige Settles held that
seat continuously through the year). Nolan Miles -- chair, Public Relations
Committee, appointed 11 February 2014. Noland Miles -- chair, Student
Affairs Committee, appointed 8 October 2013. Kept as two separate people:
the spellings appear in different meetings six months apart with no text
connecting them, and merging two people who share a name is exactly the
error this project's editorial rules warn against. Tyler Scaff -- chair,
Campus Improvements Committee, appointed 1 April 2014, succeeding Baker.

Barrett Greenwell, also appointed a senator and Student Affairs chair on 11
February 2014 per the same minutes, was already on record under
`organization.executive` (Herald, 26 Feb 2014) and the merge script correctly
skipped him as a duplicate -- the new source corroborates the existing entry
rather than adding a new person.

Rejected: Luke Coleman, whose only mention in the entire corpus is a single
line about being "moved" between committees, with no swearing-in, vote
record, or committee byline anywhere else. Too thin to stand as a seat.

## 2014-15 (13th Senate, President Nicki Taylor)

A byproduct of downloading the misfiled `/2014/` batch above. Went from 23
recorded members to 29. Elicia Tillis (senator, then Cultural and Diversity
Affairs chair), Brian Chism (Student Affairs chair), Thomas Poole, Daniel
Aroh, Dedais Hudgins and Sophie Whorf (all appointed senators by unanimous
vote or consent). None of the six meetings that name them state a calendar
date in the document's own body text -- dates in the merged entries are
hedged to "spring 2015" or a filename-implied date, not asserted as
confirmed. Sophie Whorf's own minutes contradict themselves (the narrative
says she was appointed; the boilerplate "Appointments:" summary line at the
bottom of the same document says "None") -- kept on the strength of the
specific, uniquely-worded sentence over the boilerplate line, and the entry's
note discloses the contradiction rather than hiding it. Dedais Hudgins'
given name is unusually spelled; flagged, not corrected, per the project's
own rule on spelling doubts.
