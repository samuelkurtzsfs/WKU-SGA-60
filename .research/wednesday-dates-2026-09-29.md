# The Wednesday cluster, worked through 29 September 2026

Tuesday. The third pass of 29 September corrected eight such dates, established the fault was
systematic and counted 166 further candidates; 158 remained after those eight. Each article was
fetched and read for a statement of when the business happened. A date moved only where the
article says the day.

Eighty moved. Every one lands on a Tuesday. The last column is the shortest fragment of each
article that carries the day; the article itself is linked from the entry in `years.json`.

## Read and left alone

Three entries an automated sweep would have moved and should not:

- **2016-10-12**, the two resignations amid the racism complaint. The article names a Tuesday
  meeting in its fourth paragraph but says in its second that both resigned Wednesday morning.
  The third pass left it for that reason; read again from the page and left again.
- **2017-02-08**, the Safe Ride Thursday cut. The article reports the decision as a fact and
  never dates it; its only Tuesday is an unrelated senator confirmation.
- **2019-10-02**, the 17-15 vote to cancel a meeting. The only Tuesday named is 8 October, the
  meeting being cancelled.

Four entries name Wednesday and mean it: Ransdell's email to faculty (2015-03-04), the
election closing after midnight (2017-04-19), the Campus Safety Panel (2018-02-28) and the
censure meeting that removed three senators (2024-11-06).

One entry, Ransdell's visit to SGA (2011-10-05), says the meeting happened "tonight" and never
names the weekday. Inference would move it; inference is not evidence, so it stands.

The remaining Wednesday-dated entries were read and left because no article states a day. Six
of them mention only the *following* week's meeting, which is how the first reading of this
set missed 2016-03-02, 2017-04-12 and 2021-03-03 — all three state their own Tuesday plainly
and are among the eighty.
