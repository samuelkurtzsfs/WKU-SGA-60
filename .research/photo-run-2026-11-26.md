# Photograph run, 26 November

## One new portrait: Amelia Tucker, 2026-27 Senator

Before re-running the exhausted 155-person officer gap, checked the freshest edge of the
record instead: the current 2026-27 executive and senate officers (all already portrayed) and
the four recorded senate members. Three of the four already carry a portrait; the fourth,
Amelia Tucker (Senator, also Parliamentarian of the 26th Senate), did not.

Her own `src` in `years.json` is a 28 April 2026 Herald article with no usable photograph of
her. A WP-JSON search of wkuherald.com for her name turned up "Homecoming queen candidates on
the crown" (10 Nov 2026), which profiles each of the 2026 homecoming court by sponsoring
organization. Her entry reads, captioned under her own photograph: "WKU senior Amelia Tucker,
agriculture major, is sponsored by the Student Government Association." Single-subject,
in-focus, full-length portrait credited to Natasha Gomez; downloaded from
`wkuherald.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/111026_ameliatucker_NG_1-800x1200.jpg`, confirmed
`FF D8` JPEG, 800x1200, before saving as `data/photos/2026-27-amelia-tucker.jpg` and adding to
`data/photos.json` under `leaders` (matched by name onto the senate `members` list by the
overlay merge). Build and `check_data.py` both run clean; the portrait attaches on her officer
page.
