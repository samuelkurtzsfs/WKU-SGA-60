# 30 September 2026 (photograph routine) — a working route to Talisman page images, and eight names it did not confirm

## What was checked before doing anything

Re-derived from `data/years.json` and `data/photos.json` directly rather than trusted from the
standing notes: all 73 leader records carry a portrait, Nick Todd, Katie Dawson, Jeanne Johnson
and Reagan Gilley among them; all 61 years carry at least one photograph; 1994-95, 1995-96,
2000-01 and 2008-09 are the only years with a leader portrait but no year-scene photograph, exactly
as the prior report names them. Every president and student regent has a portrait — nothing in
priority 1 or 2 of the standing brief was open. The remaining named president/regent list came back
empty on a direct scan, so this run moved to priority 3 (officers without a portrait) and priority 4
(the four thin years).

## The Cloudflare block, retested

`digitalcommons.wku.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi` is still behind a Cloudflare interstitial: a direct
request for the SGA-photographs finding aid (`article=1619&context=dlsc_ua_fin_aid`) returned
HTTP 403, `cf-mitigated: challenge`, a 5,995-byte "Just a moment..." page — matching the 25 August
diagnosis exactly, landing pages still open at 200. `web.archive.org` still resets at the TLS
handshake. Neither route opened during this session.

## A route that does work: archive.org's own image API

`archive.org/download/<item>/<item>_djvu.txt` was already known to be open and unthrottled. Two more
pieces of the same host are also open and were not being used:

- **`https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/<identifier>$<leaf>/full/full/0/default.jpg`** returns the actual
  page scan as a JPEG, at full resolution, no Cloudflare challenge, no digitalcommons dependency at
  all. Confirmed on `talisman1975west` (leaf 112, matching the frame CLAUDE.md already cites for the
  Tom LaCivita photograph) and repeatedly on `talisman1978west` and `talisman1981west`. A region can
  be requested directly (`/left,top,width,height/`) instead of the full page, which is how a single
  photo was pulled off a page of several.
- **`https://<server><dir>/fulltext/inside.php?item_id=<id>&doc=<id>&path=<dir>&q=<query>`** — the
  book reader's own search-inside endpoint — returns the surrounding OCR text and, critically, the
  scan's **leaf number** for each hit. `<server>` and `<dir>` come from `archive.org/metadata/<id>`
  (fields `server` and `dir`). Quoted multi-word queries do exact-phrase matching; unquoted queries
  match any word in the query and return noisy results, so always quote. This is a locate-then-fetch
  pair: search-inside finds the leaf, the IIIF URL fetches it, and nothing ever touches
  digitalcommons.

This closes nothing on its own — it only reaches Talisman years archive.org actually holds, which a
full identifier listing (`archive.org/advancedsearch.php?q=identifier:talisman*west`) gives as
**1943, 1946, 1947, 1963, 1964, 1965, 1971 through 1981, 1986, 1987** — 19 items. The first six
predate SGA's 1966 founding and the standing 1967-70/1982-85 gap is confirmed unchanged; nothing new
there. But it is a second, independent way to reach the 1971-81/1986-87 span this file already knew
about, and it does not depend on whether `viewcontent.cgi` is having a good day.

## Eight officers tried against it, in years archive.org holds

Cross-referencing `data/years.json`'s `organization` blocks against `data/photos.json`'s portrait
list turned up eight executive/Senate officers, across five years, who fall inside archive.org's
window and have no portrait on file: Vern Pulman (1974-75, Representative-at-Large), David Bass
(1977-78, Activities VP), David Young and Alice Wicks and Steve Wilson (1978-79, Administrative VP /
Secretary / Judicial Council Chairman), Mark Chesnut (1980-81, Treasurer), Chris Millay and Dwight
Austin (1986-87, Parliamentarian / Sergeant-at-Arms). Every name was run through search-inside
against its year's Talisman. None produced a portrait that clears this project's identification bar.
What actually came back:

- **Vern Pulman** — no match at all in `talisman1975west`.
- **David Bass** — a real hit, and the closest of the eight: leaf 38 of `talisman1978west` (printed
  page 34) carries a photo captioned "A LIGHT MOMENT IN AN ASG MEETING brings laughter from
  president Bob Moore and smiles from activities vice president David Bass, secretary Sharon May and
  vice president Cathy Murphy." The photo shows four people, two of them men, with no positional cue
  ("at left," "seated," etc.) distinguishing Bob Moore from David Bass. Read both ways and could not
  settle which of the two men in tank tops and shirts is which. **Not used** — this is exactly the
  "misidentified face is worse than no face" case the brief warns about, not a near miss to push
  through.
- **David Young** — one hit, in running text on printed page 289 of `talisman1979west` ("David
  Young, administrative vice president, said this was done to 'force some heads-up competition'..."),
  on a page whose only photographs are of scattered newsletters and an uncaptioned classroom scene.
  A real quote, no photograph of him on the page it sits on.
- **Alice Wicks** — appears only as a bare index line ("Wicks, Alice Elizabeth"); an exact-phrase
  search-inside for "Alice Wicks" came back with zero matches, meaning the index entry points to a
  photo or list where her name does not run alongside a caption. Not pursued past that.
- **Steve Wilson** — three hits, all in unrelated contexts: coordinating a fraternity's Spring Sing
  entry, singing in its barbershop quartet, and a senior-portrait line ("STEVE WILSON, agriculture")
  that gives major but no office, so it cannot be tied to the SGA Judicial Council chairman of the
  same common name without something that actually says so.
- **Mark Chesnut** — the index sent this to printed page 234 of `talisman1981west` (leaf 238,
  confirmed against the printed folio visible on the page). That page is an intramural-sports results
  box, not a photograph, and names a "Mark Chestnut" (one letter off) winning fraternity badminton
  and racquetball — sports trivia, not an SGA record, and not even a confirmed spelling match.
- **Chris Millay, Dwight Austin** — no matches connecting either name to Associated Student
  Government in `talisman1987west`; the only "Millay" hit is an unrelated commencement photo, and
  every "Austin" hit is the university Austin Peay plays in football and volleyball.

Nothing here rises to the standard the brief sets, so nothing was added. `data/photos.json` is
unchanged by this run.

## The four thin years, retested

`talisman1995west`, `talisman1996west`, `talisman2001west` and `talisman2009west` do not exist on
archive.org — confirmed directly against `archive.org/metadata/<id>` for all four (each returns an
empty record) and against the full `talisman*west` identifier listing above, which the standing note
already implied but had not been checked this precisely. The IIIF route does not reach these four
years at all; whatever closes them still has to go through `digitalcommons.wku.edu`, which was
closed the whole session.

`python3 scripts/build.py` and `python3 scripts/check_data.py` both run clean against the unmodified

The IIIF + search-inside pair above is a real, working, unblocked route to Talisman page images for
1971-81 and 1986-87 — use it before waiting on `viewcontent.cgi`. It just did not happen to clear

did. The finding aid at `dlsc_ua_fin_aid/620` and the specific `dlsc_ua_records` leads named in the

## Editor's note, 30 September 2026

Reviewed before merge. Nothing in this run reached `data/`, so nothing here reaches the site; the
value of the file is the route it documents and the eight declines it records, and both were tested
rather than taken on trust.

**Confirmed independently.** The coverage figures are exact: 61 years, 73 leader records, every one
carrying a portrait, and 1994-95, 1995-96, 2000-01 and 2008-09 the only four years with a leader
portrait and no year-scene photograph. All eight officers carry, in `years.json`, precisely the
offices named here. The `talisman*west` listing returns 19 items and exactly the years claimed.
The four thin years' identifiers are genuinely empty. The IIIF route works: full-resolution JPEGs
came back from `talisman1975west` leaf 112 and `talisman1978west` leaf 38, and a region request
cropped a single photograph off a page of four. The David Bass, David Young, Steve Wilson, Vern
Pulman, Chris Millay and Alice Wicks findings all read as this file describes them.

**The Bass decline was right, and was checked the way the LaCivita entry in CLAUDE.md requires** —
against the page image, not the text layer. The caption sits in the right-hand column beneath the
top-right photograph, so it is correctly matched to its frame and this is not a repeat of the
stairway error. The frame holds four people and the caption names four, in a bare list with no
positional cue of any kind. Which figure is Bass cannot be settled. Correctly not used.

**Three things in this file are wrong or thin, and are corrected here rather than cut, so a later
pass has the reasoning:**

1. **The IIIF URL as written returns HTTP 302, not an image.** Redirects must be followed. Fetched
   without `curl -L` it yields a 499-byte HTML stub, which saved under a `.jpg` name is trap 7 of
   section 6 exactly — the shape that once saved a bot-check page as a PDF. Any run using this route
   must follow redirects and confirm the bytes are a real JPEG before believing it.
2. **"Every Austin hit is the university Austin Peay plays" is not true.** `talisman1987west` also
   carries personal-name Austins — a Donelson senior, and two index entries — besides the place in
   Texas. The surname was not the sweep this sentence describes. The sound ground for the decline is
   narrower and holds: no "Dwight Austin" occurs anywhere in the volume, and the only Dwight in it
   is a Dwight Scott. The decline stands; the reason given for it did not.
3. **"Not even a confirmed spelling match" undersells the Chesnut evidence.** The yearbook's own
   index reads "Chesnut, Mark Cameron 234" — the spelling `years.json` uses — and points at the page
   where the results box prints "Chestnut". The identification is reasonable. What is absent is a
   photograph: page 234 is an intramural all-sports results box. That, not the spelling, is why
   there is no portrait.

One further guard for the next run: `archive.org/metadata/<id>` answers **HTTP 200 with an empty
object** for an item that does not exist, not 404. A check written against the status code will read
every absent Talisman as present.

`build.py` and `check_data.py` exit 0; `check_contrib.py` passes. `check_duplicates.py` reports four
title pairs and one same-source pair, all of them standing state on `main` rather than anything this
run introduced, and all five judged genuinely separate events — an introduction and a defeat, a suit
planned and a suit endorsed, a position taken and a bill passed, and two pieces of distinct business
from one article.
