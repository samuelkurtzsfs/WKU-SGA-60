# In their own words

One JSON file per piece, written through the contributor editor and committed
by its author. The build renders them onto the year they belong to and onto
`site/voices.html`.

```json
{"slug": "1994-95-what-the-minutes-do-not-say",
 "title": "What the minutes do not say",
 "year": "1994-95",
 "author": "Jane Doe",
 "author_role": "President, 1994-95",
 "author_email": "jane@example.com",
 "date": "2026-08-17",
 "body": ["First paragraph.", "Second paragraph."]}
```

These are first-hand recollection, not archive research, and the site labels
them that way. They are not held to the "no source, no entry" rule that governs
`years.json`, because a person's memory of their own term is the source. They
are also not allowed to quietly contradict the sourced record: where a
recollection and the archive disagree, both stand, and the disagreement is the
interesting part.
