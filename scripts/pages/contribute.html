For people who served

Add to the record

This archive was assembled from what the university and the Herald happened to write down. The people who were actually in the room know a great deal that never reached print. If you held an office in student government, you can be given the keys to your own year.

Sign in

No password. Put in your email and a sign-in link comes back to you.

Sending…

If you have not been approved yet, this form asks for access instead and the editor is told. Either way you get the same answer, so nobody can use it to find out who has an account.

Not approved yet? Tell the editor who you are

Fill these in as well and the request arrives with enough for the editor to recognise you. All three are optional.

What you will be able to do. An approved contributor can edit the year they served: correct a date, fix a name, add something the archive missed, and write a first-hand piece about their term. Every change is saved in your name and the editor can undo any of it. The one thing the editor keeps for himself is removing a person from a year, because that needs a source rather than a memory.
The archive's one rule. Everything in the dated record carries a source, so a new entry needs a link to something published: a Herald article, minutes, a university page. Your own recollection is worth more than a footnote, and it has its own place on the site under in their own words, where it runs under your name and is not asked to prove itself.
